What is a Youth Parliament?

"A practice platform where students play MPs and party leaders. You debate real national issues, follow real parliamentary procedure, and learn how decisions are made. It is a simulation of democracy — not a debate competition."

Parliamentary Glossary

Roll Call: Attendance verification at session start.
Question Hour: Period for questioning policies.
Amendment: Formal proposal to modify a resolution.
Communiqué: Final adopted consensus document.
Point of Order: Procedural intervention on rules.
Quorum: Minimum delegates needed for a vote.
Chit: Written note passed through floor pages.
Chair: Presiding Officer moderating the house.

Lok Sabha

"Discussion on the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of Electoral Rolls and the handling of public protests in the National Capital Region: examining voter representation, democratic accountability, and the balance between public order and the right to peaceful assembly."

Session Flow:

  1. (1) Roll call + opening statements
  2. (2) Question Hour + debate — no cross-examination, no rebuttals
  3. (3) Amendments + voting
  4. (4) Summary + closing

AIPPM

"Deliberation on the Independence of the Media in India and the detention of journalists during public order operations: balancing Freedom of the Press, regulation, democratic accountability, and constitutional safeguards for press freedom."

Session Flow:

  1. (1) Roll call + opening positions by party leaders
  2. (2) Structured debate — no rebuttals
  3. (3) Consensus-building
  4. (4) Final communiqué + closing
